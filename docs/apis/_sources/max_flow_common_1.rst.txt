``maximize/5``
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

Runs the maximum flow algorithm returning the flow value and the final residual dictionary. Must be defined by the importing objects.

| **Compilation flags:**
|    ``static``

| **Template:**
|    ``maximize(Network,Source,Sink,Value,Residual)``
| **Mode and number of proofs:**
|    ``maximize(+compound,+ground,+ground,-integer,-compound)`` - ``one``

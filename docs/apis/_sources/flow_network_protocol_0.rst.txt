**protocol**

``flow_network_protocol``
=========================

Protocol for flow network construction and inspection. A flow network is a directed graph where each arc has a non-negative integer lower bound and capacity, with the lower bound not exceeding the capacity, and an integer cost.

| **Availability:** 
|    ``logtalk_load(flow_networks(loader))``

.. index:: new/2
.. _flow_network_protocol/0::new/2:

``new/2``
^^^^^^^^^

Constructs a flow network from a list of arcs. Arcs are ``arc(From, To, Capacity)``, ``arc(From, To, Capacity, Cost)``, or ``arc(From, To, Lower, Capacity, Cost)`` terms. The default cost and lower bound are zero. Vertices can be any ground term. Parallel, antiparallel, and self-loop arcs are allowed.

| **Compilation flags:**
|    ``static``

| **Template:**
|    ``new(Arcs,Network)``
| **Mode and number of proofs:**
|    ``new(+list(compound),--compound)`` - ``one_or_error``

| **Exceptions:**
|    ``Arcs`` is a partial list or contains a non-ground vertex:
|        ``instantiation_error``
|    ``Arcs`` is neither a variable nor a list:
|        ``type_error(list,Arcs)``
|    An element of ``Arcs`` is not an ``arc/3``, ``arc/4``, or ``arc/5`` term:
|        ``domain_error(flow_arc,Arc)``
|    A lower bound is not a non-negative integer:
|        ``domain_error(non_negative_integer,Lower)``
|    A lower bound exceeds the capacity:
|        ``consistency_error(arc_lower_bound_and_capacity,Lower,Capacity)``
|    A capacity is not a non-negative integer:
|        ``domain_error(non_negative_integer,Capacity)``
|    A cost is not an integer:
|        ``type_error(integer,Cost)``

.. index:: flow_cost/3
.. _flow_network_protocol/0::flow_cost/3:

``flow_cost/3``
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

Computes the cost of a capacity-valid flow, without requiring conservation or optimality. Each entry must be a ``flow(ArcId, From, To, Amount)`` term with a unique one-based arc ID, matching endpoints, and a positive integer amount. Every arc lower and upper bound must be respected, including omitted mandatory arcs. Entries may be in any order. Self-loop flows contribute their full cost.

| **Compilation flags:**
|    ``static``

| **Template:**
|    ``flow_cost(Network,Flow,Cost)``
| **Mode and number of proofs:**
|    ``flow_cost(+compound,+list(compound),-integer)`` - ``one_or_error``

| **Exceptions:**
|    ``Flow`` is a variable or partial list, or an entry has a variable field:
|        ``instantiation_error``
|    ``Flow`` is neither a variable nor a list:
|        ``type_error(list,Flow)``
|    An entry is not a ``flow/4`` term:
|        ``domain_error(flow,Term)``
|    An arc ID or amount is not an integer:
|        ``type_error(integer,Value)``
|    An arc ID or amount is not positive:
|        ``domain_error(positive_integer,Value)``
|    An arc ID is not in the network:
|        ``existence_error(arc,ArcId)``
|    An entry has incorrect endpoints:
|        ``domain_error(flow_arc_endpoints,Term)``
|    Arc IDs are repeated or an arc bound is violated:
|        ``domain_error(capacity_valid_flow,Flow)``

.. index:: flow_balances/3
.. _flow_network_protocol/0::flow_balances/3:

``flow_balances/3``
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

Returns sorted ``Vertex-NetOutflow`` pairs for every vertex, including zero balances. Positive balances indicate supplies and negative balances indicate demands. Self-loops contribute zero net outflow. Requires a capacity-valid flow with the same validation and exceptions as ``flow_cost/3``, but does not require conservation against particular supplies.

| **Compilation flags:**
|    ``static``

| **Template:**
|    ``flow_balances(Network,Flow,Balances)``
| **Mode and number of proofs:**
|    ``flow_balances(+compound,+list(compound),-list(pair))`` - ``one_or_error``

.. index:: cut_capacity/3
.. _flow_network_protocol/0::cut_capacity/3:

``cut_capacity/3``
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

Computes the capacity of the cut whose source side is the given vertex set and whose sink side is its complement. Input order is immaterial; vertices must be unique and belong to the network. Empty and full sets are allowed. Counts parallel crossing arcs individually and excludes self-loops. Requires zero lower bounds.

| **Compilation flags:**
|    ``static``

| **Template:**
|    ``cut_capacity(Network,SourceSide,Capacity)``
| **Mode and number of proofs:**
|    ``cut_capacity(+compound,+list(ground),-integer)`` - ``one_or_error``

| **Exceptions:**
|    ``SourceSide`` is a variable or partial list or contains a non-ground vertex:
|        ``instantiation_error``
|    ``SourceSide`` is neither a variable nor a list:
|        ``type_error(list,SourceSide)``
|    A vertex is not in the network:
|        ``existence_error(vertex,Vertex)``
|    A vertex is repeated:
|        ``domain_error(unique_cut_vertices,Vertex)``
|    The network contains a non-zero lower bound:
|        ``domain_error(zero_lower_bounds,Network)``

.. index:: valid_cut/5
.. _flow_network_protocol/0::valid_cut/5:

``valid_cut/5``
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

Checks a ground ``cut(SourceSide, SinkSide, Arcs)`` candidate and its reported capacity. The sides must be sorted, duplicate-free lists forming a complete disjoint partition of the network vertices, with distinct existing source and sink on their respective sides. ``Arcs`` must list every positive-capacity crossing arc in network input order, including parallel arcs. Requires zero lower bounds and fails for invalid candidates. Cut validity does not imply minimum-cut optimality.

| **Compilation flags:**
|    ``static``

| **Template:**
|    ``valid_cut(Network,Source,Sink,Value,Cut)``
| **Mode and number of proofs:**
|    ``valid_cut(+compound,+ground,+ground,+integer,+compound)`` - ``zero_or_one``

.. index:: decompose_flow/3
.. _flow_network_protocol/0::decompose_flow/3:

``decompose_flow/3``
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

Decomposes a feasible flow into a list of ``path(Vertices, ArcIds, Amount)`` and ``cycle(Vertices, ArcIds, Amount)`` terms. Path endpoints are inferred from the flow balances. Vertex lists for cycles repeat the first vertex at the end. Arc IDs are one-based input arc positions.

| **Compilation flags:**
|    ``static``

| **Template:**
|    ``decompose_flow(Network,Flow,Decomposition)``
| **Mode and number of proofs:**
|    ``decompose_flow(+compound,+list(compound),-list(compound))`` - ``zero_or_one``

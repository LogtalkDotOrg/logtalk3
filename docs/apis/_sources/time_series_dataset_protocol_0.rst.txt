.. index:: observation/2
.. _time_series_dataset_protocol/0::observation/2:

``observation/2``
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

Enumerates by backtracking the time-ordered observations. ``Index`` is the 1-based position of the observation in the series and ``Value`` is its numeric value.

| **Compilation flags:**
|    ``static``

| **Template:**
|    ``observation(Index,Value)``
| **Mode and number of proofs:**
|    ``observation(-integer,-number)`` - ``zero_or_more``

.. index:: frequency/1
.. _time_series_dataset_protocol/0::frequency/1:

``frequency/1``
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

Returns the number of observations per seasonal cycle (e.g. 12 for monthly data with yearly seasonality). Fails when the series has no declared seasonal period.

| **Compilation flags:**
|    ``static``

| **Template:**
|    ``frequency(Frequency)``
| **Mode and number of proofs:**
|    ``frequency(-integer)`` - ``zero_or_one``

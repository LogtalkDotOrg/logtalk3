``cnf_statement/2``
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

Enumerates, by backtracking, canonical CNF statements in source order and their origins.

| **Compilation flags:**
|    ``static``

| **Template:**
|    ``cnf_statement(Statement,Origin)``
| **Mode and number of proofs:**
|    ``cnf_statement(-compound,-term)`` - ``zero_or_more``

| **Remarks:**

    - Origin: Either ``source(File, Start, End)`` or ``unknown``.

``parse/2``
^^^^^^^^^^^

Parses one complete ground symbol into a canonical tagged term.

| **Compilation flags:**
|    ``static``

| **Template:**
|    ``parse(Codes,Symbol)``
| **Mode and number of proofs:**
|    ``parse(+list(integer),-term)`` - ``one_or_error``

| **Exceptions:**
|    ``Codes`` is insufficiently instantiated:
|        ``instantiation_error``
|    ``Codes`` is not a character-code list:
|        ``type_error(codes,Codes)``
|    ``Codes`` does not represent a supported ground symbol:
|        ``domain_error(asp_symbol,Codes)``
|    A numeric symbol cannot be represented as an integer:
|        ``representation_error(asp_integer)``

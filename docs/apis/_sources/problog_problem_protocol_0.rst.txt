``problog_statement/2``
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

Enumerates canonical ProbLog statements in source order together with their source origins.

| **Compilation flags:**
|    ``static``

| **Template:**
|    ``problog_statement(Statement,Origin)``
| **Mode and number of proofs:**
|    ``problog_statement(-compound,-term)`` - ``zero_or_more``

| **Remarks:**

    - Statements: Statements are fully ground and use the vocabulary documented by the ``problog_program`` object.
    - Origin: Either ``unknown`` or ``source(File, Start, End)`` where positions are positive character offsets.

``prepare_learning/4``
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

Resolves numeric or auto method/coefficient requests, collects the dataset once, and fits the lowest-RMSE concrete configuration, returning the collected series for optional replay. Normalizes the validated coefficient_grid once when a coefficient is auto, default [0.1,0.2,0.5,0.8,1.0], to ascending distinct floats; ties follow method priority and ascending alpha/beta. Numeric requests remain unchanged. Never stores the grid in effective options. Preserves caller grid and missing observation variables.

|    ``static``

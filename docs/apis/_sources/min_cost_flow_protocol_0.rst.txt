.. index:: min_cost_flow/4
.. _min_cost_flow_protocol/0::min_cost_flow/4:

``min_cost_flow/4``
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

Computes a minimum cost flow satisfying the given supplies and demands, including arc lower bounds. ``Supplies`` is a list of ``Vertex-Amount`` pairs where a positive amount is a supply and a negative amount is a demand; vertices not listed are transshipment vertices. Duplicate vertices are added. Fails if there is no feasible flow. Negative arc costs (including negative cost cycles) are supported. ``Flow`` is a list of ``flow(ArcId, From, To, Amount)`` terms with a positive amount, one per arc carrying flow, in the order of the network arcs. ``ArcId`` is the one-based position of the arc in the input list used to construct the network.

| **Compilation flags:**
|    ``static``

| **Template:**
|    ``min_cost_flow(Network,Supplies,Cost,Flow)``
| **Mode and number of proofs:**
|    ``min_cost_flow(+compound,+list(pair),-integer,-list(compound))`` - ``zero_or_one_or_error``

| **Exceptions:**
|    ``Supplies`` is a partial list or contains a variable vertex:
|        ``instantiation_error``
|    ``Supplies`` is not a list of ``Vertex-Amount`` pairs:
|        ``type_error(list(pair),Supplies)``
|    An amount is not an integer:
|        ``type_error(integer,Amount)``
|    A vertex of ``Supplies`` is not a vertex of the network:
|        ``existence_error(vertex,Vertex)``
|    The supplies and demands do not add up to zero:
|        ``domain_error(balanced_supplies,Supplies)``

``min_cost_max_flow/6``
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

Computes a minimum cost flow among the maximum flows from ``Source`` to ``Sink``. Always succeeds for valid terminals. Currently requires zero lower bounds.

| **Compilation flags:**
|    ``static``

| **Template:**
|    ``min_cost_max_flow(Network,Source,Sink,Value,Cost,Flow)``
| **Mode and number of proofs:**
|    ``min_cost_max_flow(+compound,+ground,+ground,-integer,-integer,-list(compound))`` - ``one_or_error``

| **Exceptions:**
|    ``Source`` or ``Sink`` is not ground:
|        ``instantiation_error``
|    ``Source`` or ``Sink`` is not a vertex of the network:
|        ``existence_error(vertex,Vertex)``
|    ``Source`` and ``Sink`` are the same vertex:
|        ``consistency_error(distinct_vertices,Source,Sink)``
|    The network contains a non-zero lower bound:
|        ``domain_error(zero_lower_bounds,Network)``

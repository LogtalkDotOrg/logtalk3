.. index:: collect_contents/3
.. _item_content_dataset_validation/0::collect_contents/3:

``collect_contents/3``
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

Collects and validates a nonempty catalog and its content declarations.

| **Compilation flags:**
|    ``static``

| **Template:**
|    ``collect_contents(Dataset,Contents,Kind)``
| **Mode and number of proofs:**
|    ``collect_contents(+object_identifier,-list(pair),-atom)`` - ``one_or_error``

| **Exceptions:**
|    The catalog is empty:
|        ``domain_error(non_empty_catalog,Dataset)``
|    An item identifier is a variable:
|        ``instantiation_error``
|    An item identifier is not atomic:
|        ``type_error(atomic,Item)``
|    A catalog item is declared more than once:
|        ``domain_error(duplicate_item,Item)``
|    Content declarations do not cover the catalog exactly once:
|        ``domain_error(item_content_coverage,Dataset)``
|    A content descriptor is invalid:
|        ``domain_error(item_content,Content)``
|    Content representations are mixed:
|        ``domain_error(content_representation,Content)``
|    A content list is not a proper list:
|        ``type_error(list,List)``
|    A feature or content list is not ground:
|        ``instantiation_error``
|    A sparse vector repeats a feature key:
|        ``domain_error(duplicate_feature,Feature)``
|    A sparse vector entry is not a pair:
|        ``type_error(pair,Entry)``
|    A sparse weight is not numeric:
|        ``type_error(number,Weight)``
|    A sparse weight is negative or nonfinite:
|        ``domain_error(non_negative_finite_weight,Weight)``

``canonical_contents/3``
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

Canonicalizes content declarations, preserving feature occurrences and removing zero vector weights. Fails for an empty list.

| **Compilation flags:**
|    ``static``

| **Template:**
|    ``canonical_contents(Contents,Canonical,Kind)``
| **Mode and number of proofs:**
|    ``canonical_contents(+list(pair),-list(pair),-atom)`` - ``zero_or_one_or_error``

| **Exceptions:**
|    An item identifier or feature is a variable:
|        ``instantiation_error``
|    An item identifier is not atomic:
|        ``type_error(atomic,Item)``
|    An item is declared more than once:
|        ``domain_error(duplicate_item,Item)``
|    A content descriptor is invalid:
|        ``domain_error(item_content,Content)``
|    Content representations are mixed:
|        ``domain_error(content_representation,Content)``
|    A content list is not a proper list:
|        ``type_error(list,List)``
|    A sparse vector repeats a feature key:
|        ``domain_error(duplicate_feature,Feature)``
|    A sparse vector entry is not a pair:
|        ``type_error(pair,Entry)``
|    A sparse weight is not numeric:
|        ``type_error(number,Weight)``
|    A sparse weight is negative or nonfinite:
|        ``domain_error(non_negative_finite_weight,Weight)``

``check_rated_catalog/2``
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

Checks that every rated item belongs to the content catalog.

| **Compilation flags:**

| **Template:**

| **Mode and number of proofs:**
|    ``check_rated_catalog(+list(compound),+list(pair))`` - ``one_or_error``

| **Exceptions:**
|    A rated item is absent from the catalog:
|        ``domain_error(catalog_item,Item)``

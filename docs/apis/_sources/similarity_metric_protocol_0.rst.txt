**protocol**

``similarity_metric_protocol``
==============================

Protocol for pluggable similarity metrics between two sparse vectors, used to compare two users (by their item ratings) or two items (by the ratings they received).

``similarity/3``
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

Computes a similarity score between two sparse vectors of ``Key-Value`` pairs with unique ground keys and finite numeric values, unless an implementation ignores values. Higher scores indicate greater similarity. The range and handling of empty or non-overlapping vectors depend on the implementation.

| **Compilation flags:**
|    ``static``

| **Template:**
|    ``similarity(Vector1,Vector2,Similarity)``
| **Mode and number of proofs:**
|    ``similarity(+list(pair),+list(pair),-number)`` - ``one``

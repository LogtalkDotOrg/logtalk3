**object**

``jaccard_similarity``
======================

Jaccard similarity of the observed key sets of two sparse vectors: intersection size divided by union size. Values, including zeros, are ignored. Each key must occur at most once per vector. Results range from 0.0 to 1.0; an empty union gives 0.0.

**protocol**

``stn_protocol``
================

Portable, functional Simple Temporal Networks over closed difference constraints.

| **Availability:** 
|    ``logtalk_load(simple_temporal_networks(loader))``

| **Author:** Paulo Moura
| **Version:** 1:0:0
| **Date:** 2026-10-01

| **Compilation flags:**
|    ``static``


| **Dependencies:**
|   (none)


| **Remarks:**

   - Constraints: A constraint from ``X`` to ``Y`` with weight ``Delta`` means ``time(Y) - time(X) =< Delta``.
   - Inputs: States must be opaque ground terms returned by this API. Malformed public arguments and unknown time-points fail without instantiating inputs; hand-built internal states are unsupported.
   - Numbers: Weights are finite integers or floats. Arithmetic errors propagate; floating-point results are approximate, with no implicit tolerance.
   - Bounds: Unbounded query results use the atoms positive_infinity and negative_infinity, never native numeric infinities.

| **Inherited public predicates:**
|    (none)

``remove_time_points/3``
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

Removes time-points and all incident source constraints, rebuilding once and preserving surviving node order and source identifiers. Missing labels and duplicate requests are ignored. Fails for a request containing zero or a malformed or nonground list.

| **Compilation flags:**
|    ``static``

| **Template:**
|    ``remove_time_points(STN0,TimePoints,STN)``
| **Mode and number of proofs:**
|    ``remove_time_points(+stn,+list,-stn)`` - ``zero_or_one_or_error``

| **Exceptions:**
|    Arithmetic cannot be evaluated:
|        ``evaluation_error(A)``
|    Arithmetic exceeds a backend representation limit:
|        ``representation_error(A)``

| **Remarks:**

    - Bounds: Deletion retracts incident sources, not just variables. Bounds supported through removed points may weaken or become unbounded; implied bounds are not preserved as new constraints.
    - Identity: Labels are compared by term identity. The original state and next source identifier are preserved. A request removing no existing point returns the original state.

``schedule/2``
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

Returns one finite feasible assignment as time(Point, Value) terms in declaration order, with time(zero, 0) first. Fails for invalid states without instantiating inputs.

| **Compilation flags:**
|    ``static``

| **Template:**
|    ``schedule(STN,Schedule)``
| **Mode and number of proofs:**
|    ``schedule(+stn,-list(compound))`` - ``zero_or_one_or_error``

| **Exceptions:**
|    Arithmetic cannot be evaluated:
|        ``evaluation_error(A)``
|    Arithmetic exceeds a backend representation limit:
|        ``representation_error(A)``

| **Remarks:**

    - Choice: The assignment is deterministic, not necessarily unique, earliest, non-negative, or optimal. Disconnected and absolutely unbounded points are assigned finite values.
    - Numbers: Every retained source inequality is checked after normalization using backend arithmetic. Floating-point results remain approximate, with no implicit tolerance.

Returns the componentwise earliest feasible assignment as time(Point, Value) terms in declaration order, with time(zero, 0) first. Requires every declared point to have a finite absolute lower bound. Fails for invalid states or any unbounded lower bound without instantiating inputs.

| **Compilation flags:**
|    ``static``

| **Template:**
|    ``earliest_schedule(STN,Schedule)``
| **Mode and number of proofs:**
|    ``earliest_schedule(+stn,-list(compound))`` - ``zero_or_one_or_error``

| **Exceptions:**
|    Arithmetic cannot be evaluated:
|        ``evaluation_error(A)``
|    Arithmetic exceeds a backend representation limit:
|        ``representation_error(A)``

| **Remarks:**

    - Bounds: Finite upper bounds are not required. No artificial lower bounds are introduced and the original state and source identifiers are unchanged.
    - Numbers: Componentwise minimality holds in exact arithmetic. Every retained source inequality is checked using backend arithmetic; floating-point feasibility and optimality are not exact-real guarantees and no tolerance is applied.

.. index:: try_add_constraints/4

``try_add_constraints/4``
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

Attempts a batch update and returns ``consistent(STN)`` or ``inconsistent(cycle(Sources, Weight))``. The cycle is an ordered closed path of identified source constraints with negative total weight, not necessarily a minimal conflict. Invalid inputs fail.

| **Compilation flags:**
|    ``static``

| **Template:**
|    ``try_add_constraints(STN0,Constraints,Ids,Outcome)``
| **Mode and number of proofs:**
|    ``try_add_constraints(+stn,+list(compound),-list(integer),-compound)`` - ``zero_or_one_or_error``

| **Exceptions:**
|    Arithmetic cannot be evaluated:
|        ``evaluation_error(A)``
|    Arithmetic exceeds a backend representation limit:
|        ``representation_error(A)``

| **Remarks:**

    - Identifiers: Ids identifies candidate constraints in batch order even when rejected. No state is changed; rejected identifiers may coincide with later accepted allocations from the original state.
    - Arithmetic: Backend arithmetic errors propagate. Non-finite computed sums raise evaluation_error(float_overflow); inconsistent numerical diagnostics raise evaluation_error(stn_numerical_inconsistency).

``window_interval/3``
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

Returns a finite non-degenerate marginal time-point window as i(Start, End), preserving endpoint numeric representations. Fails for unknown points, invalid states, and unbounded or singleton windows.

| **Compilation flags:**
|    ``static``

| **Template:**
|    ``window_interval(STN,TimePoint,Interval)``
| **Mode and number of proofs:**
|    ``window_interval(+stn,@term,-compound)`` - ``zero_or_one``

| **Remarks:**

    - Numeric ordering: The descriptor uses arithmetic endpoint ordering, not term ordering. It is numeric data, not a portable replacement for the ranked interval bridge.
    - Envelope: Describes marginal absolute bounds, not an event duration or joint endpoint correlations.
